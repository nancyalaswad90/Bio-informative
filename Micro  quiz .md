1️⃣ Question 1:
What is the primary factor limiting the natural speed at which microbes break down oil spills?
✅ Answer: A lack of sufficient nutrients, such as nitrogen, in the environment.

2️⃣ Question 2:
According to the video, why is a major oil spill such a complex environmental disaster?
✅ Answer: The oil forms an impenetrable layer on the water’s surface, preventing all sunlight from reaching marine life.

3️⃣ Question 3:
In addition to oil spills, which of the following is mentioned as another application for bioremediation techniques?
✅ Answer: Cleaning up soil contaminated by heavy metals.

4️⃣ Question 4:
What is presented as the main current drawback of bioremediation techniques?
✅ Answer: The process is currently very slow.

5️⃣ Question 5:
How can the efficiency of bioremediation at an oil spill be enhanced, according to the video?
✅ Answer: By adding fertilizers containing nutrients like nitrogen to stimulate microbial growth.

6️⃣ Question 6:
What is the fundamental principle of bioremediation as described in the source material?
✅ Answer: Using microorganisms to break down pollutants into less harmful substances.
